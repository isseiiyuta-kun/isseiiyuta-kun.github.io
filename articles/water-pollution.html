Water Pollution

Understanding its causes, effects, and how we can protect our water resources.

What is Water Pollution?

Water pollution happens when harmful substances contaminate rivers, lakes, oceans, and groundwater. These pollutants make water unsafe for humans, animals, and plants.

Common Causes

  • Improper waste disposal
  • Industrial waste
  • Agricultural chemicals
  • Oil spills
  • Plastic pollution

Effects

  • Loss of marine life
  • Unsafe drinking water
  • Spread of diseases
  • Damage to ecosystems

How You Can Help

  • Reduce plastic use
  • Dispose of waste properly
  • Join clean-up drives
  • Support environmental organizations
